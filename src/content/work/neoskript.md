Skript lets Minecraft server owners add features by writing plain-English scripts instead of Java.

NeoSkript is a replacement that keeps the language and rebuilds what runs it. Your `.sk` files work as they are, on Paper and on Folia, and they run faster. Nothing needs rewriting to switch.

It is in public beta and open source under GPL-3.0.
