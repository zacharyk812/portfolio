Players mine ore on floating islands, sell it, and buy their way up the mines. Each island has its own ore mob and mini-boss. Their shards and cores go into a forge for better gear, and better gear opens the next island.

The higher mines and king of the hill go to whoever can take them. The first season is winter.

Airfields is in development and not open yet. It runs on the studio's own plugins.
